# Madrid, Well Chosen

A static guide to distinctive Madrid bars, restaurants, rooftop dining, cafés and affordable street food, with a dark plum and purple theme, rose accents and softly lit photo galleries.

The guide covers 36 venues, each with at least 20 photos. Beata Pasta refers to Bilbao; Ôven refers to Gran Vía 6.

Street food and affordable quick bites are on `street-food.html`: ten exact branches with reported Google scores of 4.5+ and over 1,000 reviews, dated source links, estimated spending plans and 200 photos. All three groups include attributed guest-review paraphrases and practical caveats. Reviews are a selected sample, not a consensus; Google scores are source snapshots checked 26 October 2026.

Open `index.html` for bars and `restaurants.html` for restaurants and cafés. Keep the HTML files and `assets/` directory together. No build step, framework or API key is needed.

Each venue reads in order: name and location, photos, then description and visiting details. Venues have menu links, practical visiting notes and inline photo galleries; bars and dining venues also retain their booking guidance and December considerations. The 727 verified photos and their thumbnails are bundled under `assets/photos/`, so galleries do not depend on third-party image hosts. Original source credits remain linked. Photos may show previous décor or menus. If an image fails, the gallery skips it for that page visit and hides its thumbnail; empty categories offer a clear fallback.

`assets/photos/manifest.json` records each photo’s source URL, credit, dimensions and SHA-256 checksum. Photos were downloaded, decoded and visually reviewed on 26 October 2026. Copyright remains with the respective photographers and publishers.

Rooftop dinner budgets are editorial estimates based on linked menus, not confirmed festive-package prices. Venue information was expanded on 26 October 2026; existing ranking references were researched on 25 October 2026. Recheck official sources before booking.

For GitHub Pages, publish the root of the `main` branch. `.nojekyll` serves the HTML directly. The site contains no visitor analytics or injected advertising.
