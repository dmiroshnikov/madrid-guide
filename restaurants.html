December dining

Reserve the right experience.

Book dinner, brunch or festive tea once dates are set. For 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January, confirm opening, special menus, deposits and cancellation terms directly. Advance booking is recommended here; it is not a universal venue requirement.

Which rooftop works in December?

Book an indoor meal first; decide on outdoor drinks when the weather is clear. These are restaurant recommendations with rooftop access, not promises of an outdoor table.

01

Leña Madrid

Hyatt Regency Hesperia · Paseo de la Castellana, 57

Good choice for: Choose Leña for a sit-down steak dinner in a warm, sculptural room. It works best when grilled meat is the main reason for going. Check the weight and total price of sharing cuts before ordering; this is an indoor dinner choice, without a rooftop view.

Simple plan: book a dinner sitting, choose a vegetable starter and confirm the steak portion with the server. Request a quieter table if conversation matters; seating requests are not guaranteed.

Reservations
The official FAQ recommends booking ahead, especially weekends and holidays. Reserve once dates are settled; check festive menus and cancellation terms.
What to wear
The venue recommends smart casual. A neat shirt or knitwear, tailored trousers and tidy shoes fit the room.
Food & drinks
Grilled meats and vegetables, plus dessert, wine and cocktail menus. Review the menu for cut sizes and prices before choosing a sharing steak.
Best moment
An unhurried dinner. Brunch is also advertised; confirm availability for the chosen December date.

Consider: Choose Leña when booking. Smoked Room is a separate dining experience at the same hotel. Holiday opening and special menus need confirmation.

02

Amazónico

Salamanca · Calle de Jorge Juan, 20

Good choice for: Choose Amazónico for a lively dinner with tropical décor, sushi and grilled dishes. The room is part of the experience, so it suits a celebratory evening more than a quiet meal. Specify the main restaurant or Jungle Jazz Club when booking; they are different spaces.

Simple plan: book the main restaurant for dinner. Add the jazz club only after checking its separate seating and music arrangements. Tell the venue if a quieter table is a priority.

Reservations
Book ahead for December dinner. Specify the main restaurant or Jungle Jazz Club; confirm which space the reservation covers.
What to wear
Polished smart casual is suggested. A general mandatory code was not confirmed; ask about any event-specific restrictions.
Food & drinks
A broad menu with sushi, seafood, meats and cocktails. Use the Madrid menu link; other Amazónico cities have different offerings.
Sound & seating
A sociable setting with a separate jazz-club experience. Request a calmer table if conversation is the priority; placement is not guaranteed.

Consider: For 24, 25 or 31 December, confirm opening, any fixed menu, music arrangements, deposit and cancellation deadline directly. No holiday programme is assumed here.

03

El Patio

Four Seasons lobby · Calle de Sevilla, 3

Good choice for: Choose El Patio for an afternoon coffee, pastry or booked tea service in a grand hotel lobby. It is the easiest option here for a shorter daytime stop. Decide between a casual drink, tapas and a full tea experience before booking, because menus and seating arrangements differ.

Simple plan: reserve the specific festive tea or tapas experience if that is the reason for visiting. For coffee and a pastry, ask about walk-in seating and current prices rather than booking an unrelated dining package.

Reservations
Reservations are available. Booking ahead is recommended for festive afternoon tea; ask whether the chosen service requires a reservation.
What to wear
No formal dress code verified on the official lounge page. Smart casual is a suitable suggestion for the hotel setting.
Food & drinks
Breakfast, pastries, tapas and drinks have separate menus. Afternoon-tea photos may show previous seasonal collaborations.
Best moment
An afternoon break between walks or shopping. The official page advertises merienda from 16:00–19:00; confirm the seasonal service.

Consider: Confirm the current festive tea selection, price and seating time before reserving. A photograph of a past Christmas or pastry collaboration does not guarantee that menu will return.

04

Café Comercial

Glorieta de Bilbao, 7

Good choice for: Choose Café Comercial for breakfast, brunch or a relaxed restaurant meal in a historic café with updated interiors. It is a practical stop around Bilbao. Check whether the booking is for the restaurant or café/bar area, and whether it commits you to the brunch menu.

Simple plan: use the breakfast menu for a lighter visit, or book brunch for a longer meal. Ask which room you will sit in and whether à la carte ordering is available at that time.

Reservations
The official site offers reservations. Booking brunch or dinner ahead is sensible in December; ask about casual coffee seating separately.
What to wear
No published formal dress code found. Neat casual clothing works as an editorial suggestion; dress up further for dinner if preferred.
Food & drinks
Separate menus cover breakfast and merienda, brunch, the restaurant, the bar, wines and cocktails. Choose the menu for the space being booked.
Style & timing
The most classic café choice on this page. Daylight suits brunch and the marble-table setting; a busy service may be animated.

Consider: Confirm holiday hours and whether a special menu replaces the usual restaurant or brunch offering. A restaurant reservation may have different terms from a coffee visit.

05

Dani Brasserie

Four Seasons · Calle de Sevilla, 3 · 7th floor

Good choice for: Choose Dani for a full-service rooftop meal and close-up views of the surrounding historic rooftops. It is the most expensive rooftop option here. The indoor dining room makes it workable in December; ask for a window table and treat the terrace as a weather-dependent extra.

December seating
Book inside if warmth matters. Ask whether a window table is available and whether the terrace will be operating. A past winter setup is not confirmation for this December.
Booking & access
Reserve lunch or dinner in advance. Drinks-only visits are walk-in and depend on space. Enter via the Four Seasons at Sevilla 3 and ask for Dani on the seventh floor.
Budget per person
€90–130 estimated for a shared starter, a main, dessert and one cocktail. Menu examples: burger €41, many mains €39–55, desserts from €19, cocktails €19–21; bread/olive oil service €7.
What to order
For a straightforward meal: split croquettes, choose the Rossini burger or grilled tuna, then share dessert if preferred. These are menu-based suggestions, not personal tasting claims.
Best time
Lunch gives daylight views. Dinner from 19:00 is about the illuminated city, not a daylight sunset meal. Allow around two hours as a planning suggestion.
Dress
OpenTable lists smart casual. Neat knitwear or a shirt, trousers or a dress, and tidy shoes fit the setting; bring a coat for stepping outside.

Before paying: For 24, 25 and 31 December, ask about a special menu, the final price and cancellation terms. The festive PDF found during research refers to welcoming 2026, so its prices are not treated as confirmed for December 2026.

06

Oroya & The Roof

The Madrid EDITION · Plaza de Celenque, 2 · 4th floor

Good choice for: Choose Oroya for Peruvian sharing plates and a plant-filled indoor dining room. The outdoor Roof is adjacent, so this works as dinner with an optional terrace stop. The atmosphere and food are the stronger reasons to book; an indoor table does not guarantee a clear skyline view.

December seating
The hotel explicitly says service continues inside in cold or rainy weather. Reserve the indoor room; check terrace access on the day. The outdoor fireplace does not make every table warm.
Booking & access
Dinner reservations are recommended; drinks only are walk-in. Request “Indoor / Sala” when booking. Use the EDITION entrance at Plaza de Celenque 2 and ask for Oroya.
Budget per person
€65–90 estimated for a shared starter, a main, dessert and one cocktail. Ceviche €24–26, mains €22–36, desserts €12–14 and pisco sour €18 on the linked menu. Extras cost more.
What to order
A practical start is ceviche to share, then lomo saltado or a fish main. Pisco sour is the classic drink option. Mention raw-fish preferences and dietary needs when ordering.
Best time
Aim for dinner around 19:00–20:00 as a planning suggestion. Official pages disagree on daytime hours, so confirm before planning a lunch or afternoon visit.
Dress
Marriott lists casual. Neat smart casual is a suggested choice, with a warm layer if using the terrace. A jacket-and-tie rule is not listed.

Before paying: Book the restaurant rather than assuming a drinks visit secures a table. Ask which menu applies to the room and date, and whether a festive dinner changes the normal à la carte service.

07

Picalagartos

NH Collection Madrid Gran Vía · Gran Vía, 21

Good choice for: Choose Picalagartos for a seated meal above Gran Vía at a lower menu price than Dani. The restaurant is on the eighth floor; the open-air rooftop bar is on the ninth. That distinction matters in winter: book the restaurant and decide separately whether the weather suits a drink upstairs.

December seating
Request an indoor eighth-floor table, preferably near a window. Confirm which sheltered area is assigned. A canopy or terrace photograph is not a promise of enclosed, heated seating.
Booking & access
Reserve lunch or dinner on floor 8. Standard drinks/snacks visits to floor 9 are not reservable; a separate prepaid bottle-table option may appear. Check what you are buying.
Budget per person
€55–80 estimated for a shared starter, a main, dessert and one cocktail. Listed mains include pork €26, cod €28 and squid €30; many rooftop cocktails €13–14. Table service is listed at €3 per unit.
What to order
For a simple dinner, share burrata or artichokes, then choose fish or pork. The restaurant also publishes a lunchtime rice menu. Confirm which menu is served on the floor booked.
Best time
Book lunch for daylight views, or dinner for Gran Vía after dark. Allow extra time to reach the hotel and check in during busy December evenings.
Dress
OpenTable labels the code “de vestir” (dressy). The official site gives no detailed clothing restrictions. Choose polished smart casual and check event-specific requirements.

Before paying: A dinner reservation downstairs does not guarantee a rooftop-bar table upstairs. Ask about any entrance charge, minimum spend and weather closure for the exact date before adding a rooftop drink to the plan.

Christmas-season dining

A few details to settle early.

01

Pick the space.

Restaurant, bar, jazz club and afternoon tea can have different menus and booking arrangements, even at the same address.

02

Check the full price.

Confirm fixed festive menus, drinks, supplements and deposits. Mention dietary requirements before paying for a special service.

03

Allow some time.

Leave room around December dinner and tea bookings. Confirm arrival grace periods, table duration and cancellation deadlines.

Before booking

Will the rooftop be warm enough for dinner?

An indoor restaurant and an open-air rooftop are different seating choices. Book the indoor room for December and request a window if the view matters. Ask whether an outdoor table is enclosed and heated, and whether the booking can move inside if the weather changes. Terrace photos may show summer or a previous winter setup.

Are the pictured dishes on the current menu?

They are examples from venue and editorial photographs, including earlier menus. Use the linked menus for current selections and prices; seasonal collaborations may no longer be available.

Is smart casual mandatory?

Leña recommends smart casual; Dani’s OpenTable listing specifies it. Marriott lists casual for Oroya, while Picalagartos’ OpenTable entry says “de vestir” (dressy). Recommendations for the other venues are editorial suggestions. Check the booking confirmation for event-specific requirements.
