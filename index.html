01

Isa

Four Seasons · Calle de Sevilla, 3 · First floor

Worth going for: AvroKO’s theatrical interior pairs velvet seating and low lighting with Asian-inspired cocktails and food. The appeal is an entire evening in a polished, contemporary setting, with enough energy to feel celebratory.

Reservations
Available. Booking ahead is strongly recommended for December evenings. Ask specifically about a drinks-only table if dinner is not the plan.
What to wear
OpenTable lists smart casual. Tailored trousers or dark jeans, a shirt or knitwear, and polished shoes are practical outfit suggestions.
When to go
Regular hours: Sun–Thu 19:00–01:00; Fri–Sat until 02:00. Choose an early sitting for conversation; check the current DJ schedule.
Worth considering
A restaurant as well as a bar. The venue publishes a 1h 45m table duration for groups up to four and a 15-minute arrival grace period.

Festive dates: the venue’s Christmas page advertises special dinners on 24 and 31 December. Confirm the applicable year, menu price, deposit and cancellation terms before booking.

02

Salmón Gurú

Barrio de las Letras · Calle de Echegaray, 21

World No. 66 · 2026 · extended list ↗Europe No. 46 · 2026 ↗

Worth going for: neon, comic-book references and Asian influences make the room as expressive as the drinks. Diego Cabrera’s bar is the playful choice: inventive presentation, vivid surroundings and a sense of discovery with each order.

Reservations
Walk-ins only. The official site explicitly says no reservations. Arrive near opening and allow flexibility if there is a wait.
What to wear
No formal dress code found on the official site. Relaxed smart casual is a styling suggestion; the setting is more playful than formal.
When to go
Regular opening is 16:00 daily. Sun–Thu until 02:00; Fri–Sat until 02:30. An early visit is the suggested strategy for a flexible evening.
Worth considering
Pick this for colour, creativity and energy. Food is available, but a quiet lounge atmosphere should not be the reason to come.

Published holiday closures: 24, 25 and 31 December, plus 1 January. Recheck the official notice when finalising plans.

03

Punch Room

The Madrid EDITION · Plaza de Celenque, 2

Worth going for: dark oak, a basalt fireplace and a copper bar create a warm, intimate room. The distinctive drink is punch, offered individually or in vintage silver bowls to share. A strong choice for settling in after dinner.

Reservations
Marriott’s venue description says walk-ins only, despite generic booking links on the same page. Contact the bar to clarify any holiday exceptions.
What to wear
Marriott’s published dress code is casual. Smart casual suits the room, but a jacket requirement is not listed.
When to go
The dedicated venue page lists Tue–Sat 20:00–02:00; Marriott lists daily. Confirm the specific date, especially Sunday, Monday and holidays.
Price reference
The linked “Legends & Likes” menu lists signature punches at €18 each, VAT included. Premium classics cost more.

Christmas planning: holiday opening has not been confirmed for this guide. Call +34 919 545 488 before building an evening around it.

Seasonal plates, wines and technically inventive cocktails. Food images also show the restaurant; ask which dishes are served downstairs.

Christmas planning: no holiday schedule confirmed. Call +34 915 216 678 about the exact date and bar access. Do not assume weekend opening.

The Christmas-season playbook

A little planning. A better evening.

01

Reserve where possible.

For Isa, start checking as soon as dates are fixed. Two to four weeks ahead is a planning suggestion, not a venue rule or a guarantee. Special festive dinners deserve earlier enquiries.

02

Give walk-ins room.

For Salmón Gurú and Punch Room, plan an early arrival and keep a nearby alternative. No booking means no guaranteed seat. Avoid making a tight dinner reservation depend on getting in first.

03

Check the actual date.

Ask about 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January individually. Confirm drinks-only access, special menus, deposits and cancellation terms. Bring a warm coat for walks and possible queues.

Before heading out

Is advance booking a must everywhere?

No. Isa accepts reservations, and advance booking is a sensible priority in December. Salmón Gurú explicitly takes walk-ins only. Marriott describes Punch Room as walk-in only. Jack’s Library requires a direct enquiry to establish the current booking policy. Angelita’s bookings cover its restaurant only, so ask separately about cocktail-bar access. A special festive dinner may have separate arrangements.

What does smart casual mean here?

As general outfit guidance: a shirt or neat knitwear, tailored trousers or clean dark jeans, a dress or skirt, and tidy shoes or boots. Isa’s OpenTable listing specifies smart casual; Punch Room’s Marriott listing says casual. Outfit suggestions for the other bars are editorial advice, not verified admission rules.
